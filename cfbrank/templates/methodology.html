How these rankings work

Every team carries one number. Beat someone better than you and it goes up; lose to someone worse and it goes down. No polls, no preseason expectations, no recruiting rankings, no eye test — only results.

The mechanics

Before kickoff, the gap between two ratings implies a win probability. A 400-point edge means 10-to-1 odds. Afterward, the winner takes points from the loser: a lot when the result was a surprise, very little when it was expected. Ratings are zero-sum, so the pool of rating in the sport never inflates.

Two adjustments sit on top of textbook Elo:

  • Margin of victory. The swing is scaled by log(margin + 1). A 28-point win counts for more than a last-second field goal, but with sharply diminishing returns — the difference between winning by 35 and winning by 49 is small.
  • Favorite damping. That multiplier is shrunk when a heavy favorite wins. Without it, good teams inflate their ratings by running up the score on bad ones, and the whole scale drifts apart.

Things worth knowing

  • Non-FBS opponents are pooled. Every FCS team is treated as a single fixed-strength opponent, so scheduling a cupcake can cost you rating but can't earn you much.
  • Ratings carry over between seasons, pulled {{ '%.0f'|format((1 - config.elo.preseason_regression) * 100) }}% of the way back toward average. Rosters turn over, so last year is evidence, not gospel.
  • Elo is backward-looking by construction. It measures what a team has done, not what it would do on a neutral field tomorrow — though the projections page uses it that way anyway, which is the honest use of it.
  • Early season ratings are noisy. Through three or four games, most of a rating is still last year's number.
  • Strength of schedule here is the mean final Elo of every opponent a team faced, so it moves all year as those opponents play.

Reproducing it

Every number on this site comes out of a rerunnable script: game results in, ratings out, no manual steps. The full ranking is published as CSV and JSON alongside the page. Change a setting in config.yaml, rerun, and you get a different — equally defensible — set of rankings.
